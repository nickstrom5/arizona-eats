Arizona frontier saloons

11 saloons and old bars across Arizona that also serve food: Prescott's Whiskey Row, Tombstone's Allen Street, mountain-town saloons and historic hotel bars. Each was checked in fall 2026 against a 2025–26 source.

Whiskey Row and Allen Street

Territorial Arizona drank in saloons, and a few still stand: Prescott's Whiskey Row, rebuilt after the fire of 1900; Tombstone's Allen Street; mining-camp bars up in the mountains. Founding years on this list are the year a saloon opened at its present address, going by the place or a published history. They serve food as well as drinks, but most are bars first, so check the age rules before bringing children.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Prescott (3), Tombstone (3), Paradise Valley (1), Crown King (1), Black Canyon City (1), Phoenix (1), Flagstaff (1). The dishes we found most often: steak (3).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (its own site or menu, a dated local news story or an official tourism listing) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score. Carts and stands keep short hours and some close for the summer heat, so check before you drive.

Phoenix & Central Arizona

2 saloons, by town.

  1. El Chorro

    5550 E Lincoln Dr, Paradise Valley

    America's ClassicFrontier saloon Since 1937

    Desert lodge restaurant run since 1937, when a husband and wife bought the lodge; James Beard Foundation America's Classics honoree (2005).

  2. The Stockyards Restaurant

    5009 E Washington St, Phoenix

    Frontier saloon On the menu: steak · Since 1947

    Steakhouse and saloon that opened in 1947 on the site of the Phoenix Stockyards, the feedlot cattle baron Edward A. Tovrea established in 1919; restored in 2004.

  1. Big Nose Kate's Saloon

    417 E Allen St, Tombstone

    Frontier saloon Since 1880

    Saloon whose restaurant dates to 1880, when it opened in the lobby of the Grand Hotel; the hotel's massive original bar is the centerpiece.

  2. Crystal Palace Saloon

    436 E Allen St, Tombstone

    Frontier saloon

    Allen Street saloon and restaurant (Arizona Highways, 2026).

  3. The Longhorn Restaurant

    Frontier saloon On the menu: smoked pork ribs, steak

Northern Arizona

6 saloons, by town.

  1. Rock Springs Cafe

    35900 S Old Black Canyon Hwy, Black Canyon City

    Frontier saloon On the menu: pie · Since 1918

    Roadside café established in 1918, when Ben Warner built a store while making adobe blocks for the restaurant, store and hotel; known for its pies.

  2. Crown King Saloon and Café

    7219 Main St, Crown King

    Frontier saloonGolden Plate 2025 Since 1916

    Saloon built in 1890 in nearby Oro Belle and moved to Crown King in 1916; one of Arizona's oldest continuously running bars (Arizona Highways, 2026).

  3. The Weatherford Hotel

    23 N Leroux St, Flagstaff

    Frontier saloon

    Historic downtown hotel serving guests since 1897, with Charly's Pub & Grill and the Zane Grey Bar.

  4. Matt's Saloon

    112 S Montezuma St, Prescott

    Frontier saloon

    Whiskey Row country-music saloon with a 1,200-square-foot oak dance floor; its bar has poured on Whiskey Row since 1934 (its own site).

  5. The Bird Cage Saloon

    160 S Montezuma St, Prescott

    Frontier saloon

    Whiskey Row saloon established in 1969, with live music.

  6. The Palace Restaurant and Saloon

    120 S Montezuma St, Prescott

    Frontier saloon On the menu: steak, seafood · Since 1877

    Whiskey Row saloon on the lot where D.C. Thorne opened The Palace Saloon in 1877; rebuilt after fires in 1883 and 1900 (patrons carried the Brunswick bar out of the 1900 fire) and reopened in 1901 combined with the Cabinet Saloon. Often called Arizona's oldest bar.

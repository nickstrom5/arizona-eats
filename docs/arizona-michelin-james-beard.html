Arizona MICHELIN & James Beard restaurants

29 Arizona restaurants recognized by MICHELIN or the James Beard Foundation. The MICHELIN Guide American Southwest, new in 2026, lists 19 Arizona places (seven Bib Gourmand and 12 Recommended restaurants); 19 places hold a James Beard award, a 2026 semifinalist nod or an America's Classics award.

What counts as an honor here

Two sources, both reported as facts rather than scores. One is the MICHELIN Guide American Southwest, which first covered Arizona in 2026, as MICHELIN lists it; Arizona has no starred restaurants yet. The other is the James Beard Foundation: its awards since 2022, its 2026 semifinalists and America's Classics awards from any year (3 places). Semifinalists are labeled as exactly that, and where an honor went to a chef or a restaurant group instead of the restaurant, the line under the place says so. Reader polls, "best of" roundups and review-site stars don't count.

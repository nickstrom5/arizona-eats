Arizona Route 66 diners

The Mother Road

Arizona keeps long stretches of the old road, and its towns kept the diners, drive-ins and steakhouses that fed travelers before the interstate. Seligman, where the push to name Historic Route 66 began, and Williams, the last Route 66 town bypassed by I-40, are full of them. Hours are seasonal in the high country.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Seligman (4), Williams (4), Flagstaff (3), Winslow (3), Kingman (2), Peach Springs (1), Oatman (1), Ash Fork (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (its own site or menu, a dated local news story or an official tourism listing) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score. Carts and stands keep short hours and some close for the summer heat, so check before you drive.

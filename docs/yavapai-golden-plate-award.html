Yavapai County Golden Plate Award 2025

Yavapai County Community Health Services gives the Golden Plate Award to food establishments that went a whole calendar year without a cited priority or priority-foundation food handling violation, kept an approved food safety plan and had a certified manager on duty. The county named 246 winners for 2025; the 94 restaurants below are the ones we could match to a place to eat.

The county's award, not ours

This is Yavapai County's list, shown as the county published it in its 2025 Golden Plate announcement. We matched each name to a restaurant by name and town; schools, hospitals, day cares and grocery counters on the county's list aren't here, and a winner we couldn't match isn't either. Restaurants that didn't win weren't necessarily cited for anything. It's one year's record, not a grade.

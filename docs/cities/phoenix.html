    Family-owned Chihuahua-style taco restaurant; James Beard Award semifinalist (2026). A second shop is in Mesa.

  4. Carolina's Mexican Food

    1618 W Bell Rd, Phoenix

    Sonoran Mexican On the menu: machaca burrito, flour tortillas

    Family Mexican kitchen serving the Valley since 1968, started by Manuel and Carolina Valenzuela; known for huge fresh flour tortillas (sobaqueras) and machaca burritos. Five locations.

  5. La Piñata

    5521 N 7th Ave, Phoenix

    Sonoran MexicanChimichangas On the menu: chimichanga, enchiladas

    Bugarin family restaurant opened in 1970 serving Sonoran-style Mexican food; it calls itself "The Home of the Chimichanga" and says it was the first to top the chimichanga with sour cream, guacamole, tomatoes, onions and cheese (its own claim).

  6. Macayo's Mexican Food

    12637 S 48th St, Phoenix

    Sonoran MexicanChimichangas On the menu: chimi grande

    Valley Mexican chain dating to 1946. Founder Woody Johnson said he invented the chimichanga in 1946 at Woody's El Nido, the restaurant that became Macayo's (a claim; El Charro Café in Tucson makes a competing one). This entry is the Ahwatukee (Phoenix) location.

  7. Tatemados Tacos al Carbon

    2333 N 7th St, Phoenix

    Sonoran Mexican On the menu: carne asada tacos, taco tatemado

    Taco shop in the "heritage-driven Sonoran cooking" vein (PHOENIX magazine, 2025): mesquite-grilled carne asada with wild chiltepin peppers.

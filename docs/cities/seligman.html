  1. Delgadillo's Snow Cap Drive-In

    301 Az-66, Seligman

    Route 66Golden Plate 2025 Since 1953

    Route 66 drive-in built in 1953 by Juan Delgadillo (Angel Delgadillo's brother) and run by the family since; tenth on Lovefood.com's 2025 roadside list (Route 66 News).

  2. Highway Hot Dog

    22395 W Old Hwy 66, Seligman

    Route 66

    Opened October 2025 in the century-old Historic Seligman Sundries building; all-beef hot dogs, sandwiches, espresso and gelato.

  3. Road Kill Cafe

    22830 U.S. Route 66, Seligman

    Route 66

    Seligman Route 66 café with a tongue-in-cheek menu; on Lovefood.com's 2025 roadside list (Route 66 News).

  4. Westside Lilo's Cafe

    22855 Az-66, Seligman

    Route 66

    Route 66 café opened in 1996 by Lilo Russell and her husband, Pat; their daughter Brenda now runs it.

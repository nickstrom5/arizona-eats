Arizona chimichangas

15 restaurants for the chimichanga, the deep-fried burrito Arizona claims as its own, from carne seca chimis to birria and dessert chimis. Each was checked in fall 2026 against a 2025–26 source.

Two kitchens, one claim

Who fried the first chimichanga is an argument, not a fact. El Charro Café in Tucson, opened in 1922, says its founder Monica Flin invented it; Macayo's in Phoenix says its founder Woody Johnson did, in 1946. Both claims are on this list as claims. Whoever started it, the chimi is a burrito fried golden and crisp, usually topped with sour cream, guacamole and cheese.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Tucson (12), Phoenix (2), Tumacacori (1). The dishes we found most often: birria chimichanga (3), quesabirria tacos (3), chimichanga (3), carne seca chimichanga (2).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (its own site or menu, a dated local news story or an official tourism listing) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score. Carts and stands keep short hours and some close for the summer heat, so check before you drive.

Phoenix & Central Arizona

2 restaurants, by town.

  1. La Piñata

    5521 N 7th Ave, Phoenix

    Sonoran MexicanChimichangas On the menu: chimichanga, enchiladas

    Bugarin family restaurant opened in 1970 serving Sonoran-style Mexican food; it calls itself "The Home of the Chimichanga" and says it was the first to top the chimichanga with sour cream, guacamole, tomatoes, onions and cheese (its own claim).

  2. Macayo's Mexican Food

    12637 S 48th St, Phoenix

    Sonoran MexicanChimichangas On the menu: chimi grande

    Valley Mexican chain dating to 1946. Founder Woody Johnson said he invented the chimichanga in 1946 at Woody's El Nido, the restaurant that became Macayo's (a claim; El Charro Café in Tucson makes a competing one). This entry is the Ahwatukee (Phoenix) location.

Tucson & Southern Arizona

13 restaurants, by town.

  1. Amelia's Mexican Kitchen

    5553 E Grant Rd, Tucson

    Sonoran MexicanChimichangas On the menu: birria chimichanga, quesabirria tacos

    Tucson Mexican kitchen; chef Jose Contreras is a James Beard semifinalist; birria and chicken mini chimis.

  2. Amelia's Mexican Kitchen

    5851 N Oracle Rd, Tucson

    Sonoran MexicanChimichangas On the menu: birria chimichanga, quesabirria tacos

    Tucson Mexican kitchen; chef Jose Contreras is a James Beard semifinalist; birria and chicken mini chimis.

  3. Casa Del Rio

    1060 S Pantano Rd, Tucson

    Sonoran MexicanChimichangas On the menu: chimichanga

    Sonoran-style Mexican food since 1979, known for chimichangas.

  4. Casa Molina

    3001 N Campbell Ave, Tucson

    Sonoran MexicanChimichangas On the menu: ground beef chimichanga

    Family restaurant started in 1947 by Gilbert Molina Sr.; long, narrow, crispy ground beef chimis.

  5. El Charro Café

    311 N Court Ave, Tucson

    Sonoran MexicanChimichangas On the menu: carne seca chimichanga, carne seca

    Established in 1922 by Monica Flin; the downtown restaurant is in the family's 1896 home. The restaurant claims the chimichanga was invented here (a claim; Macayo's in Phoenix makes a competing one). Known for roof-dried carne seca.

  6. El Minuto Cafe

    354 S Main Ave, Tucson

    Sonoran MexicanChimichangas On the menu: red chile chimichanga, carne seca chimichanga

    Downtown Mexican café open since 1936 (Tucson Foodie), run by the fourth generation of the Shaar family; red chile chimichanga.

  7. La Chingada Cocina Mexicana

    110 E Pennington St, Tucson

    Sonoran MexicanChimichangas On the menu: mini chimichangas, quesabirria tacos

    Downtown Mexican restaurant with murals and Sunday mariachi; mini chimis and quesabirria tacos.

  8. La Indita

    722 N Stone Ave, Tucson

    Sonoran MexicanChimichangas On the menu: potato and egg chimichanga, mole

    Founded by Maria Garcia, who is Tarascan and lived in Michoacán; potato-and-egg chimi with mole as a topping.

  9. Mi Nidito

    1813 S 4th Ave, Tucson

    Sonoran MexicanChimichangas On the menu: birria chimichanga

    South Fourth Avenue Mexican restaurant known for its birria chimichanga.

  10. Micha's Restaurant

    2908 S 4th Ave, Tucson

    Sonoran MexicanChimichangas On the menu: chimichanga

    Known for oversized chimis; the family once made a 16-foot chimichanga.

  11. Rosa's Mexican Food

    1750 E Fort Lowell Rd #164, Tucson

    Sonoran MexicanChimichangas On the menu: ortega chimichanga

    Rosa opened in 1970 on Speedway; Ortega-style chimis and the house green sauce.

  12. St. Mary's Mexican Food

    1030 W St Marys Rd, Tucson

    Sonoran MexicanChimichangas On the menu: chile relleno chimichanga, sobaquera tortillas

    Says it is one of the last Tucson places to hand-stretch its sobaqueros (giant tortillas); chile relleno chimi.

  13. Wisdom's Café

    1931 I-19 Frontage Rd, Tumacacori

    Chimichangas On the menu: turkey chimichanga, fruit chimichanga

    Known for its shredded-turkey chimichanga and dessert fruit chimis with vanilla ice cream.
